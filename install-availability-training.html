01 — Overview
What Is This Tool?

The Install Availability web page is a real-time view of the install schedule — the same data you already see in the Google Sheet, but in a browser-based format that allows live editing, job moves, and auditing without anyone needing to touch the spreadsheet directly.

📊 Same Data as the Sheet
Pulls directly from the same Google Sheet everyone is used to. Colors and status names match exactly.
✏ Live Editing
Admins and HCAs can update status, add homeowner details, and track CaaS jobs in real time.
⇄ Move & Swap
Admins can reassign a job to a different crew or swap two jobs between crews in seconds.
📋 Full Audit Log
Every change is logged — who made it, when, what changed. Nothing disappears silently.
ℹ
The TEST environment is a safe sandbox. Writes go to cmh_availability_TEST — not the live schedule. Use it to practice until you're comfortable.

04 — Changing a Cell
How to Edit a Status

Any cell can be changed once you're logged in. Cells glow darker when you hover over them in edit mode.

  1. 1
    Log in first. Click ADMIN / HCA EDIT and enter your PIN. Cells won't be clickable until you do.
  2. 2
    Click any cell. In edit mode, cells are clickable. You'll see them darken slightly on hover.
  3. 3
    Choose a status from the dropdown. The options match the Google Sheet labels exactly.
  4. 4
    For non-sold statuses (FULL DAY AVAIL, TEAM OFF, HOLD, NOT AVAILABLE) — click Continue → and you're done. The cell updates instantly.
  5. 5
    For sold statuses (FILLED, HALF DAY AM, HALF DAY PM) — you'll proceed to the Details screen to enter HCA and homeowner info. See Section 05.
⚠
3-minute timer: Once you click a cell, a countdown starts. If you don't finish in 3 minutes, the modal closes automatically and the lock is released. Don't leave it open and walk away.
05 — Marking Sold
Entering Job Details

When you mark a slot as sold, a second screen opens to capture the job details. This is where you connect the install to an HCA and homeowner.

  1. 1
    Choose the sold type:
    Full Day Install — one job all day → 1 set of fields
    Morning Install Only — AM job only → AM fields
    Afternoon Install Only — PM job only → PM fields
    AM + PM Installs — two separate jobs → both sets of fields
  2. 2
    Select the HCA from the dropdown. These match your current HCA roster.
  3. 3
    Enter the homeowner name. Free-text field — type first or last name, whatever you'd recognize.
  4. 4
    CaaS toggle: If the job is Comfort as a Service, switch to Yes. It'll show a purple CaaS tag on the board.
  5. 5
    Click Save. The cell updates immediately with the status color, HCA name, and homeowner.
✅
If you just need to change the status without entering details, you can still cancel the details screen — the status won't save until you click Save.
06 — Moving a Job
Reassigning to a Different Crew

Admins can move a sold job from one crew to another — same week or a different week. This is useful when a crew is unavailable and you need to reassign an install.

DIV 1 Team 1
Tuesday — FILLED
→
DIV 3 Team 1
Tuesday — FULL DAY AVAIL
  1. 1
    Log in as Admin. Only Admins can move jobs. HCA logins won't see the Move button.
  2. 2
    Click the sold cell you want to move (the source job).
  3. 3
    In the status popup, click ⇄ Move to Another Crew. The Move Picker opens.
  4. 4
    Read the color coding in the picker grid:
    🟧 Orange cell = the job you're moving (source)
    🟩 Green cell = open slot you can move the job to
    🟦 Blue cell = sold slot on the same week (swap available)
    Faded = blocked, can't move there
  5. 5
    Click a green cell to move the job there. A confirmation dialog shows what will happen.
  6. 6
    Confirm. The job moves instantly. The source slot becomes FULL DAY AVAIL.
📅
Cross-week moves: Use the week tabs inside the Move Picker to switch weeks. When moving cross-week, no swap is possible — the job just relocates. Source becomes FULL DAY AVAIL.
⚠
Blocked slots (NOT AVAILABLE, NOT SCHEDULED, ON CALL rows) cannot receive a moved job. They show faded in the picker.
07 — Swapping Jobs
Exchanging Two Sold Jobs (Same Week)

When two crews both have sold jobs and you need to swap them, the tool handles the full exchange automatically — both job details trade slots completely.

DIV 1 Team 1
Smith — FILLED
⇄
DIV 2 Team 2
Johnson — FILLED
  1. 1
    Click the first sold cell (the source job you want to move).
  2. 2
    Click ⇄ Move to Another Crew. The Move Picker opens.
  3. 3
    Click a blue cell (another sold slot on the same week). Blue cells are swap targets.
  4. 4
    A confirmation dialog shows both job names and confirms they will exchange slots.
  5. 5
    Confirm. Both jobs swap completely — HCA, homeowner, and CaaS flag all trade places.
✅
Swaps are only available within the same week. Cross-week moves are one-directional only — the source slot becomes open when the job goes to a different week.
08 — Undo Feature
30-Second Reversal Window

After any move or swap, a green toast appears in the bottom-left corner. You have 30 seconds to reverse the action completely.

✓ Move complete
Reversible for 28s
  1. 1
    After a move or swap, the green Undo toast appears bottom-left with a live countdown.
  2. 2
    Click ↩ Undo within 30 seconds to fully reverse the action.
  3. 3
    After 30 seconds, the window expires. The action is permanent — but still in the Audit Log.
📋
Even after the undo window closes, every action is logged. If something went wrong, check the Audit Log to see exactly what happened and who did it.
09 — Admin-Only
HOLD Status

HOLD means a slot is tentatively reserved but not yet sold. Only Admins can set this status.

When to use HOLD
Customer is interested, consultation is pending, or a job is likely to close but not yet signed. Keeps the slot off the board without marking it sold.
HCA Restriction
If an HCA login tries to select HOLD, a blocking message appears. They must get approval from Amy or Geoff first.
⚠
HOLD is visible to everyone reading the board. Use it only when you have a real, near-close prospect on that date. Don't hold a slot speculatively.
10 — Timeout & Locks
The 3-Minute Timer & Slot Locking

To prevent two people from editing the same cell at the same time, the tool uses a slot lock. When you click a cell, it locks for you. Others will see a striped pattern on that cell and can't edit it.

⏱
Finish your selection
2:47 remaining
Finish your selection
0:45 — running out of time
Finish your selection
0:12 — almost expired!
  1. 1
    When you click a cell, a 3-minute countdown timer appears in the bottom-right corner.
  2. 2
    The timer turns orange at 60 seconds and red at 30 seconds.
  3. 3
    If time runs out, the modal closes automatically and the lock is released so others can edit that cell.
  4. 4
    Always Cancel if you change your mind — this releases the lock immediately rather than waiting for the timeout.
🚫
Don't click a cell and walk away. That slot will be locked for 2 minutes, blocking others from editing it until the timeout clears.
11 — Audit Log
Who Changed What and When

Every single change — status updates, job moves, swaps, and undos — is recorded with a timestamp, editor name, and before/after state. Nothing is ever silently deleted.

  1. 1
    Click AUDIT LOGS in the top-right corner of the availability page.
  2. 2
    Each entry shows: who made the change, when, the cell key (which crew + day), the previous status, and the new status.
  3. 3
    Moves and swaps also show the source and target keys, plus whether it was a cross-week move.
✅
If you see an unexpected status on the board, check the Audit Log first. It will tell you exactly who changed it and when — no guessing, no finger-pointing.
12 — Common Scenarios
Step-by-Step Walkthroughs
Scenario A — Admin
A crew just sold a full-day install. Mark it filled.
  • Log in with your Admin PIN
  • Navigate to the correct week tab
  • Click the cell for that crew + day
  • Select "Full Day Install — FILLED" from the dropdown
  • Click Continue →
  • Select the HCA, enter homeowner name, set CaaS if applicable
  • Click Save — cell turns red with job details
Scenario B — Admin
Crew had an AM job, now a PM job just sold too.
  • Click the cell currently showing HALF DAY AM
  • Select "AM + PM Installs — FILLED"
  • Click Continue →
  • The Morning panel is pre-filled — verify it's correct
  • Fill in the Afternoon Install fields
  • Click Save — cell updates to FILLED with both jobs shown
Scenario C — Admin
A job needs to move from DIV 1 to DIV 3 — same week.
  • Click the FILLED cell for DIV 1
  • Click "⇄ Move to Another Crew" (green button)
  • In the Move Picker, find a green cell on DIV 3 for the same day
  • Click it — confirm the dialog
  • DIV 1 becomes FULL DAY AVAIL · DIV 3 becomes FILLED with the job
  • Use the 30-second undo if you picked the wrong crew
Scenario D — Admin
Two crews need to swap their Friday jobs.
  • Click the FILLED cell for the first crew's Friday
  • Click "⇄ Move to Another Crew"
  • In the Move Picker, click the blue FILLED cell for the other crew's Friday
  • The confirmation dialog will show both homeowner names and confirm the swap
  • Confirm — both slots exchange all details instantly
Scenario E — HCA
Looking for open install slots this week.
  • Open the page — no login needed to read the board
  • Scan for green (FULL DAY AVAIL) or orange/yellow half-day cells
  • Log in with your HCA PIN to mark a slot as sold after you close a deal
  • Click the open cell → choose "Full Day Install — FILLED" → Continue → enter details → Save
Scenario F — Admin
Something looks wrong on the board — who changed it?
  • Click "AUDIT LOGS" in the top-right corner
  • Find the entry for that crew + day
  • The log shows: editor name, timestamp, previous status, new status
  • Follow up with that person directly
🧪
Always practice in the TEST environment first. The orange banner at the top of the page confirms you're in the sandbox. Writes go to cmh_availability_TEST — nothing touches the live schedule.
