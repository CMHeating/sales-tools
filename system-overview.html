Weekly Workflow
1
Monday: Geoff exports the unsold consult report from ServiceTitan as an Excel file and sends it to Claude.
2
Minutes later: Claude generates fresh versions of all four tools — Tracker, Leaderboard, Daily Brief, and Exec Summary — with live data.
3
Daily: HCAs log follow-up activity in their personal Google Sheet tab. 20 seconds per attempt, right after the call.
4
Daily: Geoff opens the Daily Brief before each 1-on-1. Every conversation is prepared, focused, and data-backed.
5
Weekly: Geoff forwards the Executive Summary to Paul and Kailana. One email, one page, full picture.
Follow-Up Timing Standard
Day 2–3
First touch — stay top of mind, answer questions
Day 7
Second touch — value reinforcement, financing options
Day 14
Third touch — uncover the real objection
Day 21
Fourth touch — last serious attempt, close or cut
