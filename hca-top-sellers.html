Current pricing These are the rates loaded in the ServiceTitan price book — what an HCA can quote and sell today. A revised set is with finance and will be reissued here once it imports.
one payment per family Furnace families are anchored to the 60k BTU model, full systems to the 3 ton. Every size in a family bills at the anchor’s payment — a 100k furnace and a 60k furnace cost the customer the same per month. Ductless is the exception: each capacity carries its own payment.
96 months, then buy out at 46.7% Equipment depreciates straight-line over 180 months, so at the end of a 96-month rental the customer still owns 84/180ths of it — buyout = MSRP × (180 − 96) ÷ 180. Verified against an executed CM Heating contract.
MSRP × 1.2 ÷ 96 = payment Holds on every anchor row. Where a row shows a smaller cash price against a larger payment, that’s the anchor doing its job, not an error.
