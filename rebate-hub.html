  • $1,000 duct sealing (requires 30% or more of supply ducts in unconditioned space).
  • $2.50 per linear foot duct insulation (ducts must be sealed first).
  • Eligibility: home must be primarily heated by natural gas and be a CNGC residential customer (rate schedule 503); a multi-zone ductless electric heat pump cannot be present. Application must be received within 90 days of the install date.

Note: CNGC also pays insulation and air-sealing measures (attic/floor/wall, bundles) — those must be performed by a CNGC qualified Trade Ally; ask before quoting.
