Equifax / Credit Decision

  • Target credit score: 640+.
  • Second-look customers do not get free months off.
  • No down payments.
  • No-go flag: bankruptcy.
  • Risk flags: three or fewer active trade lines, or collection 30+ days within three years.
  • Saturday decision team open until noon local time. Sundays closed.

Required Documents

  • Current driver’s license.
  • Signed credit authorization form.
  • Proof of ownership: active mortgage statement within last 30 days, with past due amounts paid.
  • If home is paid off: security deed or warranty deed.
  • Trust name as primary; executor of trust secondary.
  • Power of attorney only if owner is of sound mind.
  • If deed and driver’s license differ, verify maiden name / spelling first.

Refund Process

  • All refund requests need manager approval before accounting.
  • Subject line: Customer Name – Refund Request.
  • Email body should include refund total, reason, and customer link if useful.
  • Send to manager first, not accounting.
  • If approved, manager adds accounting with “Approved.”
Rebates

💵 Rebates / PUD / PSE

PUD Job Quick Calc Workflow

  • Grab attic and crawl insulation pictures immediately after discovery.
  • Complete heat load calculation early.
  • Build likely bids.
  • Text Geoff, Amy, Brittny, and Lyle asking who can run the calculation.
  • Text the group again when project is ready for calculation.

PUD Heat Pump Notes

  • Mitsubishi Central Systems must climatize the entire house.
  • Some Goodman inverters do not qualify for the inverter rebate.
  • If house is year 2000 or newer, go down half a ton in size.
  • For two-stage and inverter equipment: if calculation is 2.6, quote 3-ton. If 2.5 or below, quote 2-ton.

Heat Pump Savings Language

  • You can say: “Save up to $1,000 per year.”
  • You can say: “Savings can average over $500 per year.”
  • Benefits summary: comfort, savings, ROI, future-proofing, green/lower carbon footprint.

PSE / Targeted Electrification

  • Customers with existing natural gas forced-air furnace or boiler may not qualify for standard rebate.
  • They may qualify through PSE Targeted Electrification Pilot.
  • Existing gas furnace must be removed to qualify for certain full electrification incentives.
  • No gas furnace can remain part of the new system for those electric conversion incentives.
  • Confirm exact current program before quoting rebate.

Placement

📏 Outdoor Unit Placement / Clearance / Safety Distances

General Outdoor Unit Clearance

  • 3 feet on service side of unit.
  • 12 inches from house and all other sides of unit.
  • American Standard top-discharge units may be 6 inches from house if all other sides have 3 feet of clearance.
  • General property line setback if not otherwise listed: 5 feet.
  • Outdoor units typically sit 12 inches from house, so 8.5 feet from wall to fence is usually enough.

Nearby Object Clearances

  • Generator: 5 feet.
  • Propane regulators / fill valves: 10 feet.
  • Dryer vent: PUD requires 6 feet; otherwise recommended.
  • Gas meter: 3 feet left, right, and front.
  • Electric meter: 3 feet from front, 15 inches left or right of center.
  • Hot tub / Jacuzzi: 5 feet.
  • Exterior tankless water heater: 3 feet.
  • Direct vent water heater: do not place directly below exhaust termination.
  • Openable window: do not place in front of openable portion.

Overhead / Fan Clearance

  • Top discharge: 5 feet above unit.
  • If under deck, measure to floor boards, not lower support beams.
  • Side discharge / low profile: at least 1 foot above after installed.
  • Side discharge fan front clearance: 3 feet in front of fan.

Red Flags

  • HOA: most say not visible from street.
  • Fresh air intake: do not place directly under it.
  • Fireplace exhaust: place left or right, not directly interfering.
  • Downspout: avoid directly in front unless piped underground.
  • Decks: best not on deck, but wall-mount slightly above deck may work.
  • Ductless head requires minimum 10-foot line run to avoid noise.

Electrical

⚡ Electrical Rules / Quick Calc / Difficulty Levels

Panel Quick Calc

  • A. Identify max amps from main breaker/sticker: 200, 125, or 100 amps.
  • B. Multiply max amps by 0.80.
  • C. Add up larger circuits — anything 25 amps or higher.
  • D. Divide C by B.
  • Over 60%? Get a second opinion.
  • Under 60%? Generally okay.

Send Electrical Manager

  • Clear picture of panel.
  • Panel size.
  • Size of HP/AC going in.
  • Square footage of house.

Panel / Code Red Flags

  • Cannot work with panels in bathrooms unless adding to existing circuit.
  • Gas/propane to electric: check with electrical manager even if panel is 200 amp.
  • If adding two outdoor units, get second opinion.
  • Subpanels on detached buildings can be permitting/code hassle — get second opinion.
  • EV charger inquiries: set up free service visit with electrician.

Electrical Difficulty Levels

  • Minimal: homeowner provides electrical, or equipment swap-outs. Permits/electrician often still required.
  • Level 1 / Standard: easy crawl 3–4 feet tall, exterior access under 75 feet, or difficult access under 50 feet.
  • Level 2 / Full Day: difficult access over 50 feet, easy access over 75 feet, or air handler + HP with most work in attic/crawl.

Swap / Relocation Rules

  • Do not use “swap” for mobile/trailer homes.
  • Manufactured home outdoor unit swap needs standard electrical.
  • If existing HP lacks service disconnect, use standard electrical.
  • Can relocate HP/AC up to 15 feet and still use swap charge if clear line of sight to disconnect remains.
  • If moving around a corner, use standard or full-day electrical.
  • Swap going smaller tonnage → larger = half-day labor (Level 6).
  • Disconnect within 2 ft of the ground with no service outlet within 25' (not across a fence, on a deck, or under 6'7") = half-day labor (Level 6).
  • Moving the ODU = assume half-day labor (Level 6).
  • New AH + HP = full day. Multiple systems = full day. Ductless run over 70' = full day; under 70' linear = half day.

⚡ Electric Furnace Heat Strips

  • Watch for electric furnaces with 2 heat strips or more; check the panel circuits feeding the furnace.
  • Breaker count for the furnace heat strips should match the breaker count at the panel.
  • More breakers at the furnace than at the panel → needs a single-point-entry kit, or a new circuit for the furnace.

🔌 Wire Gauge — Rule of Thumb

  • 15–20 amp = 12 gauge (2 ton)
  • 25–30 amp = 10 gauge (3 ton)
  • 35–40 amp = 8 gauge (3.5–4 ton)
  • 45–50 amp = 6 gauge (4.5–5 ton)

📏 Length Estimating — Rule of Thumb

  • Elbows (90's / 45's) = add 3' each
  • LB = 3' · LL = 3' · LR = 3'

🔌 Single Point Entry

  • Used when only one circuit runs to the panel for an electric furnace.
  • Midea and Mitsubishi do not have these.

Labor

🛠️ Labor Timing / Project Length

Central System Labor

  • 80-80 or 90-90 furnace: half day.
  • 80-90 furnace: full day.
  • 80-90 in attic: always full day due to drip pan / condensate lines.
  • Adding HP/AC only: full day.
  • 80-80 furnace + adding AC/HP: full day.
  • 80-90 furnace + adding AC/HP: 1.5 days.

Oil Furnace Conversions

  • Oil furnace to 90 furnace: full day.
  • Oil furnace to 80 furnace: full day.
  • Large/heavy oil furnace: add extra half day.
  • Tight or inaccessible location: add extra half day or more depending on access.

Project Extensions

  • Choose normal install project first, as if extra labor was already completed.
  • Then add needed project extensions and materials.
  • Example: brand new 80% furnace plus 2 days ductwork = 2.5 days total.
  • Example: brand new 80% furnace + HP plus 2 days ductwork = 3 days total.

Ductless Wall Mounted

  • 1 zone: half day.
  • 2 zones: 1 day.
  • 3 zones: 1.5 days.
  • 4 zones: 2 days.
  • 5 zones: 2.5 days.
  • 6 zones: 3 days.
  • 7 zones: 3.5 days.
  • From 3+ zones: subtract half day if very easy; add half day if hard.
  • Add half day for additional outdoor unit.

Ceiling Cassettes / Slim Duct

  • Ceiling cassettes: add 2–3 hours per head.
  • PEAD / attic / slim duct: accessibility drives labor.
  • 4–5 registers, Level 1 difficulty: 1 day.
  • 4–5 registers, Level 2 difficulty: 1.5 days.
  • 6–10 registers, Level 1 difficulty: 1.5 days.
  • 6–10 registers, Level 2 difficulty: 2 days.
Ductless

❄️ Ductless / Mitsubishi Rules

Head Placement Rules

  • Most Mitsubishi heads are only designed to cool down to 67°F, not lower.
  • BTU sizing: square feet x 20 for roughly 8-foot ceilings.
  • Double for 16-foot ceilings; x30 if leaky; x40 if garage.
  • Do not oversize heads much past outdoor unit capacity.
  • Head units need 2-5/8 inches from ceiling minimum.
  • Need 4 inches clearance on right side for servicing.
  • Need 3–4 feet away from TV.
  • Cannot be above electrical panel.
  • 2 inches above window frame.

Refrigerant Line Rules

  • Minimum 10-foot run for refrigerant lines to avoid wheezing noise.
  • Condensate is best gravity fed.
  • Maximum run per head: 82 feet.
  • If at max length, avoid more than four 90-degree turns per head.
  • 48k/60k systems: up to 492 feet collective line length.
  • 24k–42k systems: up to 230 feet collective line length.
  • 20k systems: up to 164 feet collective line length.

Ceiling Cassettes / Head Types

  • 4-way ceiling cassettes need 22-7/16 inches between joists.
  • Long ceiling cassettes should not be more than 25 feet from exterior wall because of condensate line.
  • Long ceiling cassette minimum opening: 14-11/16 inches.
  • Measure joist bays in each room/location; older homes are inconsistent.
  • Designer head unit: only multi-port systems, not single-zone. Compatible with Hyper Heat. Up to 18k only.

MXZ / PUZ / Branch Box

  • PUZ heat pumps are 1-to-1 with air handlers; cannot add head units.
  • PUZ does not need branch box.
  • MXZ Smart Multi units must have “SM” in model number for multiple units.
  • Always include branch box with multi-port IntelliHeat projects.
  • MXZSM 36–60 with one PVFY air handler may connect without branch box if 1-to-1 only.
  • Use Mitsubishi catalog / Diamond System Builder to confirm accepted combinations.

Thermostat Notes

  • MHK2 can have backup heat, but furnace only aims to maintain 65°F from the return.
  • Nest, Ecobee, etc. can technically be used with Mitsubishi, but efficiency and functionality are reduced.
  • Customer can later install MHK2 to get full functionality.

Daikin Mini Split Notes

  • Wi-Fi connection: add AZAI6WSCDKB per head.
  • Emura heads: refrigerant lines cannot exit side like standard heads; only out the back right or left.
Equipment

🔥 Equipment-Specific Rules

Bosch

  • Mainly for pre-coiled and refrigerant-line-already-ran situations.
  • Requires existing coil.
  • No coil? No Bosch — go American Standard.
  • Used coil must be R-410a and have no prior leaks/issues.
  • Identify TXV and where it is.
  • If TXV is outside coil, quote Bosch.
  • If TXV is inside coil box but never used, Bosch may work.
  • Needs minimum 2 heat / 1 cool thermostat.
  • Sizing limited by furnace CFM and coil size.
  • If coil is 2.5 ton but heat load needs 3 ton, quote American Standard.
  • Does not qualify for rebates if no AHRI match.
  • 2-year labor warranty.

Daikin

  • Cannot side tap the air handler.

Ameristar 15 / AMS XV / Platinum 17

  • Match to American Standard coils.
  • Almost any thermostat.
  • Add to any furnace.
  • Clearance: 6 inches from back to wall, 20 inches from top, 40 inches in front of fan.

Goodman

  • Goodman furnaces can only exhaust out the top.
  • Some Goodman inverters do not qualify for inverter rebate.

Garage / Reznor Heater Notes

  • Venting always out roof.
  • Needs dedicated 110 line and breaker — standard electrical.
  • If gas line is already at exact location and exhaust is straightforward/short, may be half-day project.
  • Sizing rough note: 50 BTU per square foot.
  • May need new 4-inch B-vent, hanging bracket, propane kit, equipment rental/lift, gas piping, wall thermostat, permits.
Warranty / Permits

🛡️ Warranty, Guarantees, Permits

Warranty Transfer

  • All warranties and guarantees are transferable to new owners.
  • Goodman is not technically transferable, but CM is honoring it in-house.
  • Extended labor warranty transfer requires new homeowner to contact Goodman / Daikin support.
  • Customer cannot opt out of labor warranty and buy it later.
  • Warranty is not contingent on customer performing maintenance or changing filters.
  • Main equipment is covered; thermostats, condensate pumps, air cleaners follow their own manufacturer warranties.

Craftsmanship / Alteration Rules

  • Craftsmanship failure covers diagnostic fee and repair.
  • After job closeout, equipment is registered and customer gets manufacturer certificate.
  • If another company alters CM installation, it can void parts/labor warranty.
  • Adding heads to a ductless system by another company can compromise full system warranty.

Commercial Application of Residential Equipment

  • Manufacturer warranty becomes 5 years parts and 1 year labor.
  • CM suspends 2-year guarantee and lemon/craftsmanship guarantees for those jobs.

Permits

  • Permit inspections may occur from a few weeks after installation to a full year later.
  • Permit inspection applies only to the work being permitted.
  • Unpermitted past work in house generally is not flagged/fined because of the current permit inspection.
Sales Logic

💬 Objections / Scripts / Field Logic

Itemized Pricing Response

Use this if customer requests itemized pricing:

Open script
“Most HVAC companies are not able to provide itemized pricing, including CM Heating. The reason we don't break down pricing is because that is not how we calculate our pricing. We calculate what gross margin dollars we need to make on a job to be able to have a crew out for the day and then work backwards from there to build out packages. It's a fairly common practice in HVAC and plumbing — breakeven plus margin. That being said, what we can tell you is that we are the number one rated company in the state, which would be very hard to maintain if we weren’t priced competitively. And all of our services come with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If at any point you don't feel like you got every penny’s worth out of our services, simply call us and we will make it right or remove the system and refund 100% of your money. Fair enough?”

Reseller / Contractor Pricing

  • If contractor asks to waive tax because of reseller permit, we can do it if they provide copy of license.
  • Then remove tax.

Occasional Blind Spots

  • 4500+ square foot houses may need two systems to properly climatize.
  • Think through physical install actions, not just equipment selection.
  • Example: refrigerant lines over a lawnmower overhang may be impossible because installers cannot stand on it and ladders may be too far away.

Home Warranties

  • CM does not install and wait to be paid by home warranty company.
  • Customer must pay in full by installation day and deal with warranty company for reimbursement.
Fireplace

🔥 Fireplace Install Rules

Use this for insert vs. zero-clearance scheduling expectations.

Zero-Clearance Fireplace

  • ZC fireplaces are multi-day projects.
  • Day 1: fireplace crew performs GFP install, usually half day.
  • Construction/demo/build-back work must be coordinated separately.
  • No ZC fireplace installs on weekends when construction services are unavailable.
  • Check construction availability before promising schedule.
  • Payment is due on Day 1 when physical install is completed.

Fireplace Inserts

  • Usually half-day install crew project.
  • Construction/demo/build-back often not needed.
  • Can be scheduled based on fireplace crew availability.
  • Weekend scheduling may be possible if fireplace crew is working.
