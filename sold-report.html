Same day means that rep's recap for that date names the customer — the consult and the close happened on the same day. From an earlier lead means they filed a recap for that date and it does not name the customer, so the sale came off a lead worked earlier. Unknown means they filed no recap for that date, so nobody can say which it was. Unknown is never counted as either of the other two.

Sales come from ServiceTitan Sold Estimate Alerts; the split comes from the daily recap log. A rep who does not report makes their own sales unknown, not absent — the totals are still right.
