Weekly Workflow
1
Monday: Sales Manager exports the unsold consult report from ServiceTitan as an Excel file and sends it to Claude.
2
Minutes later: Follow up system generates fresh versions of all four tools — Tracker, Leaderboard, Daily Brief, and Exec Summary — with live data.
3
Daily: HCAs log follow-up activity in their personal Google Sheet tab. 20 seconds per attempt, right after the call.
4
Daily: Sales Manager opens the Daily Brief, selects the HCA he's meeting with, and walks in prepared.
5
Weekly: Sales Manager forwards the Executive Summary to Paul and Kailana. One email, one page, full picture.
